Understand what the log means

First-seen IDs do not prove newly published jobs. Limits and source access determine coverage. Missing salary or dates stay blank. Jobs absent from later snapshots remain in the log; closure detection is not included.

No Slack required. The download is inactive and contains no credentials or private spreadsheet IDs. Self-hosted n8n must remain running, with one execution at a time.
